## Mission Statement:
In 1988 at the World Conference on the Changing Atmosphere, it was globally recognized that humans are having a disproportionate impact on the climate [[1]](https://books.google.ca/books?id=j6-LAgAAQBAJ&source=gbs_book_other_versions_r&cad=1). It is widely considered that energy use is a major pathway to abate increasing greenhouse gas emissions that contribute to a changing climate [[2]](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.ca/books/669023/values-by-mark-carney/9780771051579). A deeper look into energy use reveals that transportation alone accounts more than 25% of global greenhouse gas emissions, and is the largest emitter in four Canadian provinces, including the two most populous – Ontario and Quebec [[2]](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.ca/books/669023/values-by-mark-carney/9780771051579), [[3]](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/231220/dq231220c-eng.htm). Clearly, there is a need to integrate sustainable transportation solutions in a seamless manner. One avenue in which this paradigm can be applied is personal vehicle use, and the use of electric vehicles (EVs).
	
The recent uptake in personal EVs reflects a growing need in widespread vehicle charging infrastructure across the country. Despite major strides in building this infrastructure, there remains a fear that an EV will not have the range required to get to one’s destination, termed ‘range anxiety’. Range anxiety is known to be the highest in individuals who do not yet own an EV [[4]](https://www.recurrentauto.com/research/ev-range-anxiety-afflicts-this-group-most). Thus, there is a need to alleviate range anxiety in all EV users, but especially for individuals considering an EV for their next vehicle. 
	
With the EV consumer base in mind, our team has built The EVent Planner – an EV-centric turn-by-turn navigation app. The app is designed to help EV users navigate any duration of travel within Canada with peace of mind. Through routing services, charger and amenity information, and out-of-range detection, range anxiety is alleviated. Thus, our tool reduces the barrier to entry within the EV market, promoting more sustainable personal transportation solutions. 

<br>

## Statement of Characteristics:
The EVent Planner is designed to be the all-in-one pre-trip planning companion for EV users across Canada. Once opened, the user is able to choose which province they are located in (or interested in), where the road network of the province will be drawn, and “caution roads” will be highlighted in red; Caution roads indicate areas where an EV of average range capability (330 km on full charge) is recommended to avoid at half charge or lower.  

The user can enter a starting location and trip destination to visualise the most effective route of travel and assess whether this route passes through any caution areas, from which alternative plans can be made. Using the calculator, a user can input the distance of a route and will be given a comparison of the emissions saved when using an electric vehicle compared to a gas vehicle. 

Map intractability allows users to click on any EV charger on the map to see information about the charger(s), such as charging level, accessibility, location and charging network. Users may also interact with the points of interest/amenities and see further details such as the company name, location and type of amenity.  

Included in the side panel are toggles to see specific types of amenities or EV charging types.  

Ultimately, The EVent Planner provides the user with the agency to be proactive in their travels and pioneer the future of sustainable personal transport.

## Instructions for Use:
1. **Open the Application:** Click on the link provided to access the application on your desktop or mobile browser. Read the disclaimer and then click “I Understand” to continue. 

2. **Select Location:** Choose your province to get started. Once in the app you can change provinces by selecting the tab at the top. 

3. **Find your Route:** Select the ‘Directions’ button on the upper left and enter your stop and stop locations. The route will draw on the map showing start and end points, and directions will be listed. 

4. **Find Chargers:** EV Charging Stations will appear on the map. If a route follows a red road, it means there are no chargers down this road, and you may run out of battery charge as there is no near charging station. Click on any of the charger points to view more specific information in the lower left panel. 

5. **Find Amenities:** Click on ‘Places to Go’ in the upper left, to find local amenities. It is defaulted to a 1 km walking range but can be adjusted. Alternatively, zoom to your desired location and use the filters on the far right to find a specific type of amenity. 

6. **Find a Station:** Select your location and click on ‘Nearby Stations’ on the upper left, to get a list of nearby stations.  

7. **CO2 Emission Calculator:** Select the calculator at the top to see how much carbon emissions are saved by using an EV instead of a gas-powered vehicle.  

8. **Government Incentives:** The Government of Canada has incentives for purchasing an EV. Click the link at the top to see more information.  

9. **Total New EVs:** The graph on the left shows the total new EVs that are registered in the province each year. No data is currently available for Newfoundland & Labrador, or Alberta.
