# 🚲 RackHero

## 🎯 Our Goal

**RackHero** is designed to make personal cycling a more reliable and practical mode of transportation by addressing one of its most overlooked challenges: finding safe and dependable bike parking infrastructure. 

By combining existing municipal data with crowdsourced user feedback, our mission is to:
* **Reduce Commuter Anxiety:** Eliminate the uncertainty of managing the final leg of cycling trips by clearly showing where safe parking is located.
* **Empower Cyclists:** Create a platform where users can discover, rate, and report the state of bike parking infrastructure across Canada.
* **Inform Urban Planning:** Generate a positive feedback loop where real-time crowdsourced data from cyclists helps shape the future of biking infrastructure and maintenance in their cities.

## 🛠️ How to Use RackHero

RackHero is designed to be seamless for both web and mobile users. Here is how you can use the app to improve your daily commute:

### 1. Find the Best Parking
* **Explore the Map:** Browse the interactive map to view bike parking locations across your city.
* **Smart Filtering:** Narrow down the map to exactly what you need. You can filter by specific wards and neighborhoods to focus on your route. 
* **Weather-Ready Routing:** On rainy days, use the shelter filter to quickly isolate covered bike racks from both official municipal sources and community additions.

### 2. Check Community Ratings
* **View Rack Details:** Select any bike rack icon on the map to open a rich pop-up.
* **Read Reviews:** Access user-uploaded photos, text comments, and community-driven ratings on *safety*, *shelter availability*, *maintenance quality*, and *crowdedness*.

### 3. Contribute and Report
* **Add & Update:** See a new rack that isn't on the map? You can seamlessly add new locations or update the details of existing ones.
* **Submit Hazard Reports:** If you arrive at a rack and find an issue, select the feature on the map and choose the **Report** option. You can quickly submit a short description (max 1000 characters) categorizing the issue as:
  * `MISSING`
  * `DAMAGED`
  * `VANDALIZED`
  * `OTHER`
* **Compare Reports:** Use the overview tab to filter all local reports by category. These filters persist as you navigate the map, allowing you to easily compare the condition of different bike racks in your area before you arrive.

## 💻 Tech Stack

This project is built heavily on the **Esri ArcGIS Ecosystem**:
* **Frontend / Main Application:** ArcGIS Experience Builder
* **Crowdsourcing / Data Entry:** ArcGIS Survey123 & ArcGIS QuickCapture
* **Backend:** ArcGIS Hosted Feature Layers
* **Data Preparation:** ArcGIS Pro
