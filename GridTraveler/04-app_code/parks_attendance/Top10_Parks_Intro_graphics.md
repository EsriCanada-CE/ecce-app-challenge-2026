# Top 10 Canadian National Parks by Visitation Rate
## Top1: Banff National Park (Visitation Rate: 4,230,156)
As Canada’s first national park, Banff offers unparalleled views of the Rocky Mountains, vibrant turquoise lakes like Lake Louise, and pristine alpine forests. It is an absolute must-see for anyone looking to experience the rugged beauty of the West.
### EV Advantage
You can easily keep your battery topped up while you explore. You will find multiple free, public Level 2 chargers (including Tesla and J-1772 connectors) at the Cave and Basin Historic Site and the Banff Visitor Centre, plus fast chargers scattered throughout the Banff townsite.
![Banff National Park](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9c026962-079e-4a80-8e87-bbcc1204daa6)
## Top 2: Fortifications of Québec National Historic Site (Visitation Rate: 2,000,488)
Step back in time by walking the historic walls of Old Québec, the only fortified city in North America north of Mexico. The fortifications offer incredible views of the St. Lawrence River and an immersive look into the colonial history of the region.
### EV Advantage
Located right in the heart of Québec City, this site benefits from the city's robust and dense public charging network. You can plug in at a nearby municipal parking garage or street charger and spend the day exploring the walled city on foot.
![Fortifications of Québec National Historic Site](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c0b09bb3-bc8d-491d-b143-0c79fc4bbdf5)
## Top 3: Lachine Canal National Historic Site (Visitation Rate: 1,438,350)
A true urban oasis in Montreal, the Lachine Canal features a stunning 14.5-kilometer path that winds through the city's rich industrial past. It is perfect for cycling, walking, or paddling, offering a scenic escape right in the middle of a bustling metropolis.
### EV Advantage
Montreal is highly EV-friendly. There is an abundance of Level 2 and fast chargers along the canal route and in surrounding neighborhoods like Griffintown and Saint-Henri, making it incredibly easy to charge up while you grab a coffee or rent a kayak.
![Lachine Canal 2](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9971c821-8d8e-44c0-b6c9-128b6b604bce)
## Top 4: Pacific Rim National Park Reserve (Visitation Rate: 1,173,986)
Experience the raw, untamed beauty of Canada's west coast. Famous for the sweeping sands of Long Beach, lush old-growth temperate rainforests, and world-class surfing, Pacific Rim is a haven for nature lovers and thrill-seekers alike.
### EV Advantage
The nearby coastal communities of Tofino and Ucluelet have embraced electric travel. You will find Level 2 charging stations at various municipal lots, resorts, and visitor centers, allowing you to recharge while you hike the rainforest trails.
![Pacific Rim National Park Reserve](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c6795f7b-4368-47fc-94f5-b85553dc21d3)
## Top 5: Saguenay-St. Lawrence Marine Park (Visitation Rate: 1,158,251)
This is one of the best places in the world for whale watching. Where the Saguenay Fjord meets the St. Lawrence River, the nutrient-rich waters draw belugas, minke whales, and even massive blue whales.
### EV Advantage
Parks Canada has equipped this marine conservation area with Tesla and J-1772 Level 2 chargers. You can confidently plug in at the Marine Environment Discovery Centre or the Pointe-Noire Interpretation and Observation Centre while you scan the horizon for breaching whales.

## Top 6: Jasper National Park (Visitation Rate: 1,142,080)
Banff’s wilder, more expansive northern neighbor offers dramatic peaks, ancient glaciers, and incredible wildlife viewing. Driving the Icefields Parkway up to Jasper is routinely ranked as one of the most scenic road trips on the planet.
### EV Advantage
Thanks to a growing network of public chargers, you can easily reach Jasper in an EV. There are Level 2 and universal fast chargers available right within the Jasper townsite, ensuring you have plenty of range to explore Maligne Lake or the Athabasca Falls.
![Jasper National Park](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fda74ad1-eb06-4dfa-90a5-ae7cd05baa6a)
## Top 7: Rideau Canal National Historic Site (Visitation Rate: 922,897)
Winding 202 kilometers from Ottawa to Kingston, this historic 19th-century waterway is a marvel of engineering and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Watching the hand-operated locks in action is a quintessential Ontario summer experience.
### EV Advantage
Parks Canada provides EV charging stations at select lockstations along the route. Additionally, both anchor cities (Ottawa and Kingston) and the charming towns dotting the canal offer excellent municipal and fast-charging infrastructure.
![rideau-canal](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/54ec012a-0001-4f3d-9561-261b3a0328af)
## Top 8: Halifax Citadel National Historic Site (Visitation Rate: 913,514)
Perched high above the city, the star-shaped Halifax Citadel offers panoramic views of the harbor and a fascinating look into Canada's military history, complete with the iconic 78th Highlanders firing the noon gun.
### EV Advantage
Parks Canada has installed dedicated Level 2 EV charging stations on-site for visitors. You can plug in right at the Citadel, ensuring your vehicle is fully charged by the time you finish exploring the fort and head down the hill for a seafood dinner on the waterfront.
![Halifax Citadel National Historic Site](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/79e9c00f-a78a-4cdb-96a6-220c9b94561d)
## Top 9: Mount Revelstoke & Glacier National Parks (Visitation Rate: 837,761)
These twin parks offer two very different but equally spectacular experiences. Drive the Meadows in the Sky Parkway in Mount Revelstoke to reach stunning alpine wildflower meadows, then head to Glacier to witness steep peaks, deep valleys, and impressive avalanche terrain.
### EV Advantage
While chargers inside the parks themselves are limited, the nearby towns of Revelstoke and Golden are well-equipped with BC Hydro fast chargers, Tesla Superchargers, and Level 2 hubs to keep you moving through the mountain passes.
![Glacier-National-Park](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/013efaa0-291d-43fe-97d2-a607d0735b88)
## Top 10: Prince Edward Island National Park (Visitation Rate: 689,386)
Famous for its warm water beaches, rolling sand dunes, and striking red sandstone cliffs, PEI National Park is a coastal paradise. It is also home to Green Gables Heritage Place, immortalized by the classic Canadian novel.
### EV Advantage
PEI National Park is highly accessible for EVs. Parks Canada provides free Level 2 charging stations (with both Tesla and universal plugs) at several key locations, including Green Gables Heritage Place, Greenwich Beach, and the Ardgowan National Historic Site.
